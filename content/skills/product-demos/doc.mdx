# Product demos

Product demos is an Agent Skill that makes a 20 to 30 second demo film of a web product, from the product's real screens, the way a video agency would for a client. Give it to Claude Code, Codex or AllyHub Studio with your product's address and who it is for; it comes back with the film, the brand book and script behind it, and a kit for the next films.

## When to use it

Use it for:

- A demo of a web app, a SaaS product or a website, for a home page, a launch or a sales deck.
- A tour of the product for new users.
- A series of demos in one brand's look, each built on what the last one made.

Use another skill for:

- How something works, in motion graphics, in about a minute: [Explainers](/skills/explainers/).
- A short vertical video for each release, for TikTok, Reels or LinkedIn: [Launch shorts](/skills/launch-shorts/).
- Finding the people who need your product: [Candidate users](/skills/candidate-users/).

## What you get

- The first film: 16:9, in English, 20 to 30 seconds, unless you ask for another length, format or language.
- A brand book with the brand's colours, fonts and voice.
- A product brief and the competitors.
- A direction for the brand's films, argued so you can say yes to it.
- The script, a row per moment, written before the film.
- A kit for the next films: the product's screens, the brand's elements, its music and sounds.
- A list of what to make next.

## What it takes

- **Time:** about an hour for the first film. Later ones are faster.
- **Cost:** the skill is free (MIT) and AllyHub runs on your computer for free. A generated voice, music or footage comes from AllyHub's cloud tools, which use credits.
- **From you:** the product's address and who it is for. A login or a test account if the product needs one.
- **On your computer:** the agent opens your product in a browser, uses AllyHub to make the film, and keeps everything in your project's folder. It never posts or publishes anything unless you ask.
- **Agents:** Claude Code or Codex with Node.js 22.19 or newer, or AllyHub Studio, which brings everything.

## How it works

1. The agent uses your product as a visitor would and studies the brand and its competitors.
2. It writes the direction and the script, then makes the film from the product's real screens.
3. You watch the film take shape in a preview, mark what to change, and the agent changes it.
4. You export the film. What it made stays in the project, so the next film starts from it.

## Questions

### Does the film show my real product?

Yes: its real screens and its real numbers, captured as the agent uses it. The agent lists the claims it inferred, so you can check them before you publish.

### What if my product needs a login?

The agent asks you for one, or for a test account. It stops to ask only for what it cannot decide.

### Can I make more films after the first?

Yes. The first round leaves a kit and a list of what to make next; each new film reuses them.
