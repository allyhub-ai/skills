# Candidate users

Candidate users is an Agent Skill that finds people who show in public that they have the problem your product solves: they ask how to do it, complain about what they use now, describe a workaround or hire for it. Give it to Claude Code, Codex or AllyHub Studio with your product; it comes back with a ranked list, each person with the post that shows it and why they fit, and keeps searching round after round.

## When to use it

Use it for:

- Early users, beta testers or people to interview.
- Leads and prospects who already have the problem.
- Finding where your users talk: Reddit, LinkedIn, X, TikTok, forums and more.

Use another skill for:

- A demo film of your product: [Product demos](/skills/product-demos/).
- How something works, in about a minute: [Explainers](/skills/explainers/).
- A short video for each release: [Launch shorts](/skills/launch-shorts/).

## What you get

- A ranked list of candidates, a row per person or company, with columns for your own status and notes, which the agent never writes in.
- The signals: each post that shows the problem, in its author's own words, dated.
- A report for each round: who to contact first and why, what each source found, what people said, and what the next round will try.
- The searches: where, what was asked, what it found, and whether it is worth running again.
- A product brief, the competitors, and a profile of who to look for and who is not a fit.

## What it takes

- **Time:** about an hour for the first round, which aims at about 50 people, ranked.
- **Cost:** the skill is free (MIT). It searches with AllyHub's research tools, which need an AllyHub account; most searches cost a fraction of a credit.
- **From you:** the product. Who it is for and where they talk help; otherwise the agent works them out and says what it assumed.
- **What it never does:** contact anyone, guess an email or collect anything private. It uses public posts as they were posted, and a page that needs a sign-in is noted, never worked around.
- **Agents:** Claude Code or Codex with Node.js 22.19 or newer, or AllyHub Studio, which brings everything.

## How it works

1. The agent reads the product and writes who to look for, the signals that give them away and where they talk.
2. It plans the searches and picks the tool that does each best.
3. It searches, adds the people and the posts it finds, and ranks them.
4. It writes the round's report. You decide who to contact; the next round starts from what this one found.

## Questions

### Does it contact anyone?

No. It finds and ranks people; you decide who to contact.

### Where does it search?

X, Reddit, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, app and product reviews, job posts and more, with AllyHub's research tools. A forum or a blog that no tool reaches it reads through its public pages, at a polite pace.

### Does it get better each round?

Yes. Each round starts from the profile, the people and the searches the last one kept, and notes which searches are still worth running.
