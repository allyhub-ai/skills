# Launch shorts

Launch shorts is an Agent Skill that makes a 15 to 30 second vertical video for each release of a product: what is new, why it matters and where to get it, sized for where it will be posted. Give it to Claude Code, Codex or AllyHub Studio with what is launching; it keeps the release history, so each short starts from the look and the openings that worked.

## When to use it

Use it for:

- A video for each release or new feature, or a changelog video.
- A teaser for TikTok, Reels, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn or X.
- A run of launch videos in one look, release after release.

Use another skill for:

- A demo film of the whole product: [Product demos](/skills/product-demos/).
- How something works, in about a minute: [Explainers](/skills/explainers/).
- Finding the people who need your product: [Candidate users](/skills/candidate-users/).

## What you get

- The short: 9:16, 15 to 30 seconds, unless you ask for something else. Each platform's version keeps to its size, safe zone, length limit and loudness.
- Its plan, with the release notes it was made from, proposed before it is made.
- The release history: a row per release with its date, what launched, where it was posted, and its views and how it did once you say.
- The look, its colours and fonts, once you have agreed on it.

## What it takes

- **Cost:** the skill is free (MIT) and AllyHub runs on your computer for free. Built-in sound effects cost nothing; a generated voice or music comes from AllyHub's cloud tools, which use credits.
- **From you:** what is launching (the notes, the changelog or the page) and where it will be posted.
- **On your computer:** the agent looks at the new feature in your product, as a customer would, makes the short with AllyHub and keeps everything in your project's folder. It never posts or publishes anything unless you ask.
- **Agents:** Claude Code or Codex with Node.js 22.19 or newer, or AllyHub Studio, which brings everything.

## How it works

1. The agent reads what is launching, whole, and captures the new feature in the product if it can reach it.
2. It proposes the short in a few lines and makes it once you agree.
3. You watch it take shape in a preview, mark what to change, and export it.
4. It adds the release to the history. When you tell it how the short did, the next one starts from what worked.

## Questions

### Which platforms does it make shorts for?

TikTok, Reels and YouTube Shorts at 9:16 unless you say otherwise. For LinkedIn, a square or a vertical version.

### Does it post the short?

No. You export it and post it yourself.

### Does each short learn from the last?

It reads the release history before every short: the look, the openings that worked and what was already said, and the views once you have told it.
